Test Cases for SCRUM-2: As a bank customer, I want to transfer money via UPI ID so that I can send funds instantly
==================================================

Here are the comprehensive test cases for the given user story:

**POSITIVE TEST CASES**

1. TC-01 | POSITIVE | Successful Transfer via UPI ID | 
    * Steps: 
        1. Login to the banking app
        2. Navigate to the transfer money section
        3. Select UPI ID as the transfer method
        4. Enter a valid recipient UPI ID
        5. Enter a transfer amount between ₹1 and ₹1,00,000
        6. Confirm the transfer
    * Expected Result: The transfer is successful, and the recipient receives the funds within 30 seconds. An SMS and in-app notification are sent to the sender.

2. TC-02 | POSITIVE | Multiple Successful Transfers | 
    * Steps: 
        1. Login to the banking app
        2. Navigate to the transfer money section
        3. Select UPI ID as the transfer method
        4. Enter a valid recipient UPI ID
        5. Enter a transfer amount between ₹1 and ₹1,00,000
        6. Confirm the transfer
        7. Repeat steps 3-6 multiple times, ensuring the daily transfer limit is not exceeded
    * Expected Result: Each transfer is successful, and the recipient receives the funds within 30 seconds. An SMS and in-app notification are sent to the sender after each transfer.

3. TC-03 | POSITIVE | Transfer Amount at Minimum Limit |

3. TC-13 | EDGE | Failed Transaction Auto-Reversal | 
    * Steps: 
        1. Login to the banking app
        2. Navigate to the transfer money section
        3. Select UPI ID as the transfer method
        4. Enter a valid recipient UPI ID
        5. Enter a transfer amount between ₹1 and ₹1,00,000
        6. Confirm the transfer
